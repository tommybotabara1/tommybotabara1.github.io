# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, recruiters, technical leads, and business stakeholders evaluating Tommy Botabara for technology consulting, data automation, AI workflow, or business-facing technical delivery roles. They are scanning quickly for credible proof that Tommy can structure practical systems, explain technical work clearly, and bridge business needs with implementation.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Tommy as a technology consultant and data automation engineer who builds practical data, automation, and AIOS-style systems. Its main job is to show shareable evidence of how Tommy thinks and builds without exposing confidential previous-job work. Success means a visitor understands the positioning within one minute, sees concrete folder-skeleton examples, and has a clear path to contact Tommy.

## Brand Personality

Practical, credible, systems-minded. The tone should feel calm and professional, but not bland. It should make Tommy look like someone who can organize messy work, automate repeatable processes, and explain technical delivery in business language.

## Anti-references

This should not look like a generic frontend portfolio, a crypto or hacker landing page, a loud AI SaaS template, a resume pasted into a webpage, or a page that claims confidential client outcomes it cannot show. Avoid overclaiming, vague AI buzzwords, excessive terminal cosplay, and decorative effects that distract from the evidence.

## Design Principles

1. Show systems, not just claims.
2. Make private-work constraints feel intentional, not like a gap.
3. Prefer concrete artifacts, folder structures, scripts, and workflows over generic project summaries.
4. Balance business credibility with technical texture.
5. Keep every interaction useful: filters, skeleton tabs, and console panels should clarify what was built.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, keyboard navigation, focus states, and responsive behavior. Preserve reduced-motion support. Avoid relying on color alone to communicate state. Keep copy readable on mobile and do not hide core content behind hover-only interactions.
